### In short

- Smooth and reassuring donation flow focused on campaigns
- A donor area for history and tax receipt management
- A complete back-office: CRM, receipts, payment synchronization, automation...

### The need

Humainement Concernés needed to professionalize their online fundraising. The association was operating with a basic website and manual processes that were hindering its development. They needed a tool capable of reassuring donors on one side, and structuring the team's management on the other: donation tracking, history, automated receipts, and campaigns.

### Our approach

We needed something simple, quick to set up, and scalable. So we opted for a dedicated web application.

On the donor side, the experience was redesigned to reduce friction: the cause for each campaign is clear, selecting an amount is simple, and creating an account allows them to effortlessly access their history.

On the association side, we developed a custom CRM connected to HelloAsso and PayPal. The team manages its campaigns, tracks payment statuses in real-time, and generates tax receipts from a single secure interface. A truly healthy database to support their growth.

#### What changes for the user

The donor is guided without any doubt or distraction, from discovering the campaign to making a payment. Logging into their account gives them total autonomy over their personal information, recurring donations, and downloading their receipts.

### Technically

A modern and robust architecture to guarantee security and speed: React & TanStack Start (Yes, TanStack Start!) for instant navigation, a fast-executing backend via Bun, and a PostgreSQL database managed with Drizzle. Authentication is handled with Better Auth and payments (HelloAsso/PayPal) are automatically synchronized via background requests. (cron)

### Results

The association went from tedious manual management to an automated ecosystem. The NGO's campaigns now have a premium showcase, donors give with more confidence, and the team saves precious time on accounting and issuing tax receipts.

### What's next

The platform was built to last and evolve. It now allows them to confidently plan for growth in annual donation volume, the addition of major new campaigns, and the deployment of more effective follow-up strategies.
